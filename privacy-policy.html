basics. Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective 17 November 2026.

Who operates basics.

Jerson Ombina operates basics., an offline speedcubing timer. The app does not require an account.

App data and use

basics. stores solve times, scrambles, penalties, session names and settings on your device to show history and statistics and remember your preferences. The app does not upload these records to an operator server. It does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Exports and Android backup

You can create a JSON backup and choose where to store or share it. The destination handles its copy under its own rules. Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device and settings, Android may copy app records and settings to your backup account or transfer them to another device.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or clear app storage. Use the app to delete solves or sessions and Android Settings to clear the app’s data. Manage exported files and Android backups separately in the locations where you keep them.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub handles those records under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email support, we use your address and the information you send to respond. We keep support correspondence as needed to resolve your request, follow up on related issues, or meet legal obligations.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. In the Philippines, you can also raise a concern with the National Privacy Commission. If you are a parent or guardian with a question about a child’s information, use the contact below. Contact Jerson Ombina at basics.timer.app@gmail.com.
