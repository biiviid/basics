basics. is a speedcubing timer application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data.

All data (solve times, scrambles, session information) is stored locally on your device. No data is sent to any server or third party.

Internet Connection

basics. does not require an internet connection to function. The app does not communicate with any remote servers.

Third-Party Services

basics. does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any personal information, the app is safe for users of all ages.
